Privacy

VaaniLens is a hackathon prototype. This page explains exactly what it stores and how to have it removed. It reflects the spirit of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

What we store

  • To use the live demo: the LinkedIn profile URL you enter, plus your approximate city, region/country, device (mobile/desktop) and browser.
  • If you join the waitlist: your name, email, business type and (optional) city — only because you asked us to contact you.
  • Anonymous usage counts: which features (transcribe, speak) were used, by city and device.

What we do NOT store

  • No IP addresses.
  • No full browser user-agent strings.
  • No call audio is kept after it is processed. All demo calls are synthetic.

Why

So we can see who tried the prototype during the hackathon and whether it's useful. That's it. We do not sell or share your details with anyone.

Delete my data

Email us and we'll delete your LinkedIn URL and any waitlist entry: officialzingro@gmail.com.

Not affiliated

VaaniLens is built on Gnani Prisma and Gnani Timbre but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Gnani.ai.
