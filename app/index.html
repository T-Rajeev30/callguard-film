How it works

Listen. Understand. Respond.

Three stages, one Indian voice stack. Prisma hears the call, VaaniLens reads the intent, Timbre speaks the reply.

01

Gnani Prisma listens

Speech-to-text built for code-mixed Hinglish — the way India actually talks on calls.

Prisma · codemix transcript

02

VaaniLens understands

It finds the commitment and how strong it is, deadlines like "kal" or "agle hafte", plus confusion, disputes and follow-ups.

Signals

03

Gnani Timbre replies in Hindi

Text-to-speech answers in a natural Indian voice — no robotic translation, no English accent bolted on.
